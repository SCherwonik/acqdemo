# Civilian Acquisition Workforce Key Performance Objectives (KPO) Library

Model Objectives by Functional Area and Cross-Functional Workforce Behaviors

## How to use this library

This library provides model KPO objectives. Enterprise KPO categories are mandatory, but supervisors should tailor each objective's performance expectations, measures, performance indicators, and timeframe to the employee's role, grade, duties, mission context, and applicable performance management system. The library is a tool to support implementation; it does not replace component HR policy, performance management procedures, or the forthcoming OUSW(P&R) HR-community communication.

## 1. Purpose

This KPO Library provides model Strategic Outcome Objectives and Workforce Behavioral Objectives for civilian members of the warfighting acquisition workforce. The objectives are intended to support implementation of FY26 NDAA Section 826 and to align workforce performance with warfighter-focused mission outcomes, acquisition speed, innovation, responsible risk-taking, and continuous learning.

## 2. Application

Each covered acquisition workforce member must have at least one Strategic Outcome KPO and one Workforce Behavioral KPO incorporated into the FY 2027 performance plan and appraisal process. Supervisors should use the objectives below as examples and tailor them to the employee's primary duties, position level, mission context, and performance management system.

- Strategic Outcome KPOs describe what mission or organizational outcome the employee is expected to support or achieve.
- Workforce Behavioral KPOs describe how the employee is expected to perform, including acquisition judgment, collaboration, innovation, responsible risk-taking, end-user engagement, and continuous learning.
- KPO performance information should be documented, verifiable, and tied directly to both outcomes and behaviors.
- KPO performance information may inform assignments, promotions, awards and recognition, training, certification, and continuous learning, consistent with applicable law, policy, HR guidance, and component procedures.

Note: model functional area KPOs may be adapted for use across other acquisition functional areas, as appropriate.

## 3. Model Functional-Area KPOs

### Auditing and Oversight

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Provide timely oversight that protects resources while enabling mission execution and outcome-focused acquisition. |
| Strategic Outcome | Apply risk-based oversight that balances mission needs, compliance requirements, and operational efficiency. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Identify and communicate high-risk issues early with actionable recommendations that support timely corrective action. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Collaborate with acquisition teams and stakeholders to resolve findings efficiently while maintaining independence and professional standards. |

### Business - Cost Estimating

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Deliver timely and accurate cost estimates that support rapid, informed acquisition decisions. |
| Strategic Outcome | Identify major cost drivers early and recommend options to mitigate affordability, schedule, or execution risks. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Use data-driven analysis to recommend scope changes, trade-offs, or termination of misaligned efforts when increased costs or risk exceeds acceptable thresholds. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Benchmark costs against relevant commercial, historical, or analogous data where appropriate to improve decision quality. |

### Business - Financial Management

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Align funding execution to support timely capability delivery while maintaining fiscal discipline and transparency. |
| Strategic Outcome | Provide timely financial analysis that enables informed trade-offs, prioritization, and execution decisions. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Maintain clear visibility into cost, schedule, funding, and resource execution to support responsible decision-making. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Identify financial approaches that remove barriers to innovative, commercial, or adaptive acquisition when appropriate and permissible. |

### Contracting

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Reduce procurement lead time and improve mission responsiveness through streamlined acquisition strategies and appropriate use of innovative authorities. |
| Strategic Outcome | Expand access to commercial and nontraditional sources through effective market research, solicitation design, and engagement strategies. |
| Strategic Outcome | Improve award quality and execution discipline by reducing avoidable rework, delays, post-award corrections, or acquisition friction. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Propose and implement contracting approaches that improve speed, competition, affordability, or mission outcomes while maintaining required accountability. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Collaborate with program teams, end users, legal, and resource stakeholders to translate mission outcomes into effective contract structure. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Use data and performance insight to identify cost, schedule, quality, or vendor-performance risks early and recommend corrective action. |

### Engineering and Technical Management

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Accelerate technical maturation, integration, or verification activities that support timely delivery of operational capability. |
| Strategic Outcome | Identify and integrate innovative, commercial, digital, or emerging technologies that improve operational effectiveness or acquisition outcomes. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Use data-informed analysis, experimentation, modeling, data, and trade-off analysis to support technical decisions, including adjustment or discontinuation when warranted. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Incorporate end-user feedback and operational context into technical priorities, designs, and recommendations. |

### Life Cycle Logistics

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Improve readiness through integrated sustainment planning, supportability analysis, and execution aligned to operational needs. |
| Strategic Outcome | Identify and mitigate sustainment, obsolescence, supply-chain, or industrial-base risks that affect mission readiness. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Adjust sustainment strategies using performance data, operational feedback, cost insight, and mission priorities. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Evaluate and apply commercial product support or sustainment solutions when they improve readiness, affordability, or mission outcomes. |

### Program Management

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Accelerate delivery of operational capability by applying disciplined tailoring, empowered decision-making, and execution focus appropriate to the program's mission need. |
| Strategic Outcome | Identify and apply commercial solutions, adaptive acquisition pathways, or innovative authorities when they improve speed, affordability, or operational relevance. |
| Strategic Outcome | Integrate supply-chain resilience, industrial-base considerations, and surge capacity into program planning and execution where relevant. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Engage operational users and stakeholders regularly and incorporate feedback into requirements, priorities, trade-offs, and execution decisions. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Use iterative planning, data, and trade-off analysis to recommend continuation, adjustment, acceleration, or termination of efforts when warranted. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Identify, assess, document, and manage responsible risk-taking options using appropriate authorities and decision channels. |

### Test and Evaluation

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Design and execute test strategies that accelerate learning, reduce uncertainty, and support timely delivery decisions. |
| Strategic Outcome | Provide decision-makers with operationally relevant test results that inform capability, risk, and readiness decisions. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Adjust test plans based on results, user feedback, program reality, and evolving mission needs. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Engage operational users in defining test priorities, evaluating outcomes, and identifying mission-relevant information needs. |

## 4. Model Supervisory KPO Consideration

The following model objective may be used or adapted for supervisors of warfighting acquisition workforce personnel when it aligns with the supervisor's duties and applicable performance management system. If leadership intends this objective to be mandatory for all covered supervisors, that requirement should be stated in the implementing memo or supervisor guidance.

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Supervisory Model Objective | Monitor acquisition workforce certification and continuous learning status for direct reports, intervene early when compliance or development risks are identified, and support timely completion of required learning consistent with Department and component requirements. |

## 5. Cross-Functional Innovation and Mission Outcome KPO Menu

The following model objectives may be applied across functional areas to reinforce innovation, speed, mission impact, and responsible risk-taking. Supervisors should tailor expectations to the employee's role and scope of responsibility.

| Type | Model Objective |
|---|---|
| Strategic Outcome | Commercial-First Consideration: Demonstrate consideration of commercial solutions or services before bespoke development when mission-appropriate. |
| Strategic Outcome | Innovative Authority Utilization: Apply appropriate innovative authorities, adaptive pathways, or streamlined approaches when they improve mission outcomes. |
| Strategic Outcome | End-User Outcome Focus: Engage end users and incorporate operational feedback into acquisition decisions, planning, and trade-offs. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Iteration and Trade-Offs: Use iterative cycles, performance insights, and data-driven trade-offs, including adjustment or termination of misaligned efforts when warranted. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Digital and AI Literacy: Complete role-relevant digital, data, or AI learning and apply it to improve acquisition execution, decision quality, or mission outcomes. |
| Workforce Behavioral | Responsible Risk-Taking: Identify, assess, document, and manage risk-informed options that accelerate outcome delivery while preserving accountability. |

## 6. KPO Tailoring and Performance Information Notes

Supervisors should adapt model KPOs using SMART criteria and should identify performance information that is appropriate to the employee's responsibilities. Useful performance information may include measurable outcomes, work products, stakeholder feedback, documented decision rationale, demonstrated application of competencies, training outcomes, or mission impact indicators.

**Recommended tailoring formula**

[KPO category or model objective] + [role-specific task or behavior] + [measurable outcome/results] + [timeframe or performance cycle].
